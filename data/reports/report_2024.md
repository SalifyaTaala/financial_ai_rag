# Annual Report 2024

## Overview

Revenue for 2024 was USD 10.5 million. Our subscription business grew steadily through the year, while our services business stayed stable. Operating costs for 2024 were USD 4.2 million, mainly staff salaries and office rent. We ended the year with USD 3.1 million in cash.

## Financial highlights

| Item | 2024 (USD million) |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | 10.5 |
| Cost of sales | 3.9 |
| Operating costs | 4.2 |
| Operating profit | 2.4 |
| Interest expense | 0.2 |
| Profit before tax | 2.2 |
| Income tax (20%) | 0.44 |
| Net income | 1.76 |

## Revenue by segment

Subscriptions generated USD 6.3 million of revenue in 2024, and services generated USD 4.2 million.

| Segment | 2024 (USD million) |
| --- | --- |
| Subscriptions | 6.3 |
| Services | 4.2 |
| Total revenue | 10.5 |

## Operating costs breakdown

Staff salaries were the largest part of operating costs in 2024. Office rent and other costs, such as software and travel, made up the rest.

| Cost | 2024 (USD million) |
| --- | --- |
| Staff salaries | 2.5 |
| Office rent | 0.9 |
| Other | 0.8 |
| Total operating costs | 4.2 |

## Cash flow

We started 2024 with USD 2.5 million in cash. Operations produced USD 2.2 million. We spent USD 0.6 million on equipment and USD 1.0 million on loan repayments and dividends, which left us with USD 3.1 million at the end of the year.

| Item | 2024 (USD million) |
| --- | --- |
| Cash at start of year | 2.5 |
| Cash from operations | 2.2 |
| Equipment spending | -0.6 |
| Loan repayments and dividends | -1.0 |
| Cash at end of year | 3.1 |

## Outlook

We plan to hire more people in the sales team during 2025 and expect revenue to keep growing. The main risk is that a small number of customers account for a large share of subscription revenue. We do not publish revenue forecasts.
