# Annual Report 2025

## Overview

Revenue for 2025 was USD 12.0 million, up from USD 10.5 million in 2024. Operating expenses rose to USD 4.8 million in 2025, mainly because of hiring in the sales team. We ended the year with USD 4.4 million in cash.

## Financial highlights

| Item | 2025 (USD million) |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | 12.0 |
| Cost of sales | 4.5 |
| Operating expenses | 4.8 |
| Operating profit | 2.7 |
| Interest expense | 0.2 |
| Profit before tax | 2.5 |
| Income tax (20%) | 0.50 |
| Net income | 2.00 |

## Revenue by segment

Subscriptions generated USD 7.8 million of revenue in 2025, and services generated USD 4.2 million.

| Segment | 2025 (USD million) |
| --- | --- |
| Subscriptions | 7.8 |
| Services | 4.2 |
| Total revenue | 12.0 |

## Operating expenses breakdown

Staff salaries increased the most in 2025, because of the new sales hires. Office rent and other expenses were unchanged from the previous year.

| Expense | 2025 (USD million) |
| --- | --- |
| Staff salaries | 3.1 |
| Office rent | 0.9 |
| Other | 0.8 |
| Total operating expenses | 4.8 |

## Cash flow

We started 2025 with USD 3.1 million in cash. Operations produced USD 2.9 million. We spent USD 0.7 million on equipment and USD 0.9 million on loan repayments and dividends, which left us with USD 4.4 million at the end of the year.

| Item | 2025 (USD million) |
| --- | --- |
| Cash at start of year | 3.1 |
| Cash from operations | 2.9 |
| Equipment spending | -0.7 |
| Loan repayments and dividends | -0.9 |
| Cash at end of year | 4.4 |

## Outlook

We expect the new sales team to support continued revenue growth. The main risk is still that a small number of customers account for a large share of subscription revenue. We do not publish revenue forecasts.
